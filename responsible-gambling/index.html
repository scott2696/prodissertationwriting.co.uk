Responsible Gambling: Every Tool Available in the UK

If gambling has stopped being entertainment, help is free and immediate. The National Gambling Helpline is on 0808 8020 133, 24 hours a day, every day, and it is confidential. Get help now

National Gambling Helpline — 0808 8020 133. Free, confidential, 24 hours a day, run by GamCare. Live chat is available at gamcare.org.uk. For advice and treatment referral, BeGambleAware. For NHS treatment, ask your GP about the National Gambling Treatment Service, which takes self-referrals. If you are in crisis, call Samaritans on 116 123, free, 24 hours a day.

The questions worth asking yourself

Gambling harm is not a single event; it arrives gradually and it is easiest to address early. None of the following is a diagnosis, but each is worth being honest about:

  • Are you gambling with money set aside for something else?
  • Have you gambled to win back losses, and then again after that?
  • Are you gambling for longer, or for more, than you intended to?
  • Have you concealed how much you gamble from someone close to you?
  • Do you feel restless or irritable when you try to cut down?
  • Have you borrowed money, or sold something, to gamble?
  • Have you looked for a site that would let you play after being blocked elsewhere?

That last one is worth sitting with on a site like this one. If you arrived here because a UK-licensed operator restricted your play, or because GamStop blocked you, the block was working, and looking for a way past it is itself the signal.

The combination that actually holds

No single tool is complete. The combination that works, and that is worth setting up in one sitting, is: GamStop (covers everything UK-licensed), plus Gamban or BetBlocker on every device you own (covers everything else, including every site named on this website), plus your bank's gambling block (stops the payment even if a site gets through). Each closes a gap the others leave open, all three are free or near-free, and together they take about twenty minutes to put in place.

GamStop, in detail

GamStop is free, run by an independent not-for-profit, and takes a few minutes. You provide your name, date of birth, address, email and mobile, and choose a minimum period of six months, one year or five years. Every UK-licensed online operator is then required to block you.

Two things people routinely misunderstand. It cannot be cancelled early — once the minimum period is set, it runs, and that is the whole design rather than an inconvenience. And it does not expire automatically: at the end of the period the exclusion continues until you actively ask for it to be lifted, after which a seven-day cooling-off applies.

What it does not cover: land-based gambling, the National Lottery, and any operator without a UK licence — which includes every site written about on this website. That gap is exactly why the device-level blockers above matter.

Limits you can set today

  1. Set a deposit limit at every site you use. All of them offer one, including the offshore sites, and none of them require you to. Set it while you are calm; nobody sets a sensible limit mid-session.
  2. Turn on your bank's gambling block. Card controls in your banking app. Note that most banks impose a 24 to 72 hour cooling-off before it can be turned off again, which is the point.
  3. Install a device blocker. Gamban or BetBlocker, on your phone, your tablet and your computer. GamCare funds Gamban free for many users.
  4. Register with GamStop if UK-licensed sites are part of the picture.
  5. Turn off marketing at every operator, and unsubscribe from every gambling email. Promotional messages are designed to reach you at the worst moment.
  6. Tell one person. The single most effective step, and the hardest. Harm grows in secrecy.

Four beliefs that cost people money

“I am due a win.” Games have no memory. A slot that has not paid in two hundred spins is exactly as likely to pay on the next spin as it was on the first. The feeling that a win is owed is the most expensive intuition in gambling.

“I can win it back.” Chasing turns a defined loss into an undefined one. Every bet placed to recover is made at the same house edge as the bets that created the deficit, and usually at a larger stake.

“I am good at this.” On casino games there is no skill that overcomes the house edge; on betting, skill exists but is much rarer than people believe, and a winning month is not evidence of it. Keeping a written record of every bet is the fastest cure for this belief.

“A bigger bonus means better value.” Almost the opposite. Larger headline offers generally carry heavier wagering terms, and on our modelling most cost more to clear than they are worth — which is set out in pounds on the bonuses page.

If it is someone else's gambling

Support exists for family and friends, not only for the person gambling. GamCare runs a dedicated service for those affected by someone else's gambling, Gam-Anon holds support groups, and the National Gambling Helpline on 0808 8020 133 takes calls from concerned relatives as well as from gamblers.

Practical steps that help: do not pay off gambling debts directly, as it usually delays rather than resolves the problem; consider separating finances; and help the person set up the blocking tools above rather than policing them yourself.
