Cookie Policy

Written by The PoundPlay editorial desk · Fact-checked by Priya Raman · Updated 13 September 2026

This site uses as few cookies as it can. Nothing but strictly necessary cookies is set unless you consent, and you can change your mind at any time.

What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file a website stores in your browser, used to remember something between page loads — a preference, a session, or an analytics identifier. Similar technologies such as local storage work the same way and are covered by this policy.

This site sets no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, runs no ad network, and does not build advertising profiles. There are no social media tracking pixels.

Affiliate cookies, specifically

When you click through to an operator, that operator or its affiliate network sets a cookie on their domain to record that you arrived from here, so a resulting account can be attributed to us. That cookie is set after you leave this site, is controlled by them, and is governed by their cookie and privacy policies. Its typical lifetime is between 30 days and 12 months depending on the network.

How to control cookies

  • On this site: use the cookie settings link in the footer to change or withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing consent stops analytics cookies being set and you can clear existing ones in your browser.
  • In your browser: every major browser lets you block or delete cookies, in Settings under Privacy. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may stop parts of the site working properly.
  • Browser privacy modes discard cookies when the window closes, which means you will be asked for a cookie preference on each visit.

Changes to this policy

If we add or remove a cookie, this page is updated and the date at the top changes. See also our privacy policy and terms and conditions. Questions: privacy@prodissertationwriting.co.uk.
