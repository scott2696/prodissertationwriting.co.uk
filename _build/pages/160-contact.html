What we cannot help with

We are not an operator, a regulator or a dispute-resolution service. We cannot access your casino account, release a withdrawal, reinstate a voided bonus or make an operator pay you. If you are in dispute with a casino, the route is: the operator's own formal complaints process first, then a complaint to the authority named in its licence. If you are self-excluded through GamStop and want help, please contact the National Gambling Helpline on 0808 8020 133 rather than us — they are free, confidential and available 24 hours a day.

Sending a correction

The most useful corrections include three things: the page URL, what specifically is wrong, and where the correct information can be checked — a screenshot of current terms, a regulator's register entry, or a dated withdrawal confirmation.

What happens next: we verify it against the source, and if it stands up we change the page and add a dated correction note. If we disagree, we reply and explain why. We do not ignore corrections, and we do not quietly amend a page and pretend it always said that.

If you are in dispute with an operator

We cannot resolve it, but we do want to know about it, because a pattern of complaints about a casino listed here is a reason for us to re-test it or remove it. Useful details:

  • The operator, and the date the problem started
  • What you asked for and what they said
  • Whether your account was fully verified at the time
  • Whether a bonus was active on the balance
  • Any reference number from the operator

Meanwhile, the practical steps that actually work: put the complaint in writing to the operator and ask for a formal complaint reference; keep every chat transcript and screenshot; and if you get no resolution, escalate to the regulator named in the site's footer. The non-GamStop page explains what each offshore regulator's complaints process is realistically worth.

For operators and affiliate managers

We are happy to hear from operators, and we will correct factual errors about your site immediately on the same terms as anyone else. Two things it is fairer to say up front than to discover later:

  • Scores are computed from five published weights. We cannot adjust one without changing a criterion score, and we will not change a criterion score without evidence.
  • Paid placement is available and is labelled as an advertisement, with the operator's real score shown beside it. We do not sell ranking positions unlabelled, and we do not remove critical content as part of a commercial arrangement.
