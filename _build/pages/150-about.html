Why this site exists

Search “best online casinos” and you will find a hundred pages that look broadly identical: a list of operators, a five-star graphic beside each one, a paragraph of copy that could describe any of them, and a bonus figure in bold. Almost none of those pages tell you where the ranking came from. Almost none price the bonus they are advertising. And almost none has ever had money inside the cashier they are recommending.

We started this because we wanted two specific numbers that nobody publishes:

  • What a welcome bonus actually costs to clear, in pounds, derived from the operator's own terms — rather than the headline percentage, which is the number chosen for the advertisement.
  • How long a withdrawal really takes, timed from pressing the button to cleared funds, from accounts we opened and funded ourselves.

Both are on this site, both are computed rather than typed, and both are what the rankings are built from. The method is published in full on the review methodology page, and the payout evidence is in the withdrawal ledger.

How we make money, precisely

When you open an account at a casino through a link on this site, that operator pays us a share of its revenue from your play — typically between 30% and 50%, depending on the operator. We are paid nothing when you click, nothing when you register without depositing, and nothing at all by operators we do not list.

What we will not do

  • We will not write a score we cannot derive. Scores are computed from five published weights at build time. There is no field in this system where a number can simply be typed.
  • We will not invent a payout figure. If we have not made the withdrawal, the ledger says so rather than estimating.
  • We will not quote terms we have not read. Where an operator does not publish terms we can model, the clearance table says so rather than guessing a multiple.
  • We will not describe an offshore casino as safe. They are less protected than UK-licensed sites, and every relevant page says so in the same words.
  • We will not market to anyone who is self-excluded. Several pages here open by asking people on GamStop to leave, which costs us traffic and is the right thing to do.
  • We will not quietly remove a bad review. If an operator leaves the site, the page says why.

When we get something wrong

We will. Operators change bonus terms without notice, licences move between registers, and payout performance drifts. When something here is wrong, the fix is to correct it and date the correction on the page rather than to edit it silently.

If you find an error — a stale wagering multiple, a licence that has changed, a payout claim that does not match your experience — please tell us through the contact page. Corrections from readers are the most useful feedback this site gets, and they are acted on.

Editorial standards

  1. Everything carries a named author and a named fact-checker. Nothing is published anonymously or under “the editorial team” except this site's legal pages.
  2. Every factual claim is sourced to something checkable — a statute, a regulator's register, an operator's published terms, or a measurement we made ourselves.
  3. Numbers are computed, not asserted. Scores and bonus clearance costs are derived at build time from published inputs.
  4. Commercial relationships are disclosed on every page, not only here.
  5. Responsible gambling messaging appears on every page, including the ones where it costs us conversions.
  6. Pages are reviewed on a schedule, currently monthly, with the last review date and the next one shown on the page.
