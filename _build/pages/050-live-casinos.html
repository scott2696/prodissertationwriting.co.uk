Are live dealer games fair?

Can I count cards at a live blackjack table?

Practically, no. Most live tables use a continuous shuffle machine or reshuffle around half the shoe, which removes the deck penetration counting depends on, and operators monitor bet-spread patterns and may restrict accounts. Basic strategy is entirely different: it is simply optimal play, it is expected rather than penalised, and it takes the house edge down to roughly 0.5%.

By roughly eighty times at the same stake. How do live dealer games compare to RNG casino games?

The outcome of a live game is a physical event — a real card, a real wheel — streamed to you, where an RNG game is a certified random number generator producing a result on a server.
