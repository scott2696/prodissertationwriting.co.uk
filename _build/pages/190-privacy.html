Privacy Policy

This site collects very little personal data, because it does not need much. This policy explains what is collected, why, for how long, and what rights you have under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller for this site is the operator of prodissertationwriting.co.uk. For any data protection question, or to exercise any right described below, contact privacy@prodissertationwriting.co.uk.

We do not collect: names unless you give us one, payment details, gambling account details, date of birth, or any special category data. We have no login, no user accounts and no newsletter.

3. Analytics

Analytics run only if you consent through the cookie banner. Where analytics are used, IP addresses are truncated or anonymised before storage where the provider supports it, and we do not use analytics data to identify individuals or to build advertising profiles. You can withdraw consent at any time through the cookie settings link in the footer.

4. Affiliate links and what operators see

When you click a link to an operator, that operator and its affiliate network record the click — typically a referral identifier, a timestamp, your IP address and your browser details — so that a resulting account can be attributed to this site.

Once you leave this site, you are on the operator's site and their privacy policy applies, not ours. We receive aggregate and account-level reporting from affiliate networks about registrations and revenue attributed to us. We do not receive your name, your contact details, your payment details or details of your individual gambling activity, and we never seek them.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. Data may be processed by service providers acting on our instructions: our hosting provider, our analytics provider (only with your consent), and our email provider. Each processes data only for the purposes we specify. We may also disclose data where legally required.

6. International transfers

Some providers process data outside the United Kingdom. Where that happens, transfers are made under the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, or on the basis of an adequacy decision.

7. Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you have the right to: access the personal data we hold about you; have it rectified if inaccurate; have it erased; restrict or object to its processing; receive it in a portable format; and withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these, email privacy@prodissertationwriting.co.uk. We respond within one month. Given how little we hold, an access request will usually return very little — which is the intention.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data you may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

8. Security

This site is served over HTTPS. It is a static website with no database of user data, no user accounts and no payment processing, which removes most of the ways personal data is usually lost. Contact email is held in a standard business email account with access limited to the two people named on our authors page.

9. Children

This site is intended for adults aged 18 or over and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last changed, and material changes will be noted on this page.
