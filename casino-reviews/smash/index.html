The bonus, which is the entire argument

Smash advertises 600% total up to £10,000 casino / 250% up to £5,000 sports with wagering of 10x on deposit plus bonus. The base matters — deposit plus bonus is a larger turnover base than bonus alone — but at a multiple of ten it is still a fraction of what the field charges. A 35x offer on the bonus alone requires more than three times the turnover of this one.

Priced on a £100 first deposit: a £200 bonus, £3,000 of required turnover, an expected cost at 96% RTP of £120, and an expected net of +£80. Every 35x offer on this site comes out negative on the same model. That comparison is the reason Smash is first, and you can check the arithmetic yourself against the full clearance table.

The surrounding terms are clean rather than clever. Maximum bet while wagering is £5, which is standard. Live games count 10%, which is also standard and means the bonus is a slots product. There is no maximum-conversion cap buried in the promotion, which there very often is.

Who this offer suits

Anyone who plays slots and intends to clear a bonus rather than decline one. At 10x, clearing is a realistic project rather than a theoretical one — which is not something I can say about most of the offers on this website.
