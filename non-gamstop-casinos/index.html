What GamStop is, and what it is not

GamStop is the national online self-exclusion scheme for Great Britain. You register once, choose a period of six months, one year or five years, and every operator holding a UK Gambling Commission remote licence is required to block you for that period. It is free, it is run by an independent not-for-profit, and it is a licence condition rather than a voluntary scheme.

Three things it is not, which cause most of the confusion:

  • It is not a register of bad operators. A site being “not on GamStop” says nothing about its conduct. It says only that it does not hold a UK licence.
  • It does not cover land-based gambling — betting shops, casinos, arcades all operate separate schemes.
  • It cannot be cancelled early. Once the minimum period is set, it runs. That is the entire design, and it is the reason it works.

So “non-GamStop casino” is not a category of casino. It is a description of a licensing status, and it happens to correlate with the commercial features people are searching for because the same regulatory gap produces both.

The legal position

Checked against the Gambling Act 2005

It is not an offence for an adult in Great Britain to gamble at an overseas online casino. The Gambling Act 2005 imposes its requirements on those who provide facilities for gambling, which is why section 33 creates an offence of providing facilities without a licence — an offence committed by an operator, not by a customer. There is no corresponding player-side offence. Separately, an unlicensed operator advertising to British consumers may breach section 330; again, the liability sits with the advertiser.

What that means in practice is that your risk is commercial rather than legal. If a site refuses to pay you, you have not broken any law, but the body that would normally take up your case has no jurisdiction over the operator. Your route is a complaint to the licensing authority named on the site's footer — which brings us to how much that licence is actually worth.

Non-GamStop casinos: your questions

What are non GamStop casinos?

Online casinos that do not hold a UK Gambling Commission licence. Participation in GamStop is a condition of that licence, so any operator licensed elsewhere — Curaçao, Anjouan, or Malta serving other markets — sits outside the scheme automatically. It is a description of licensing status rather than a type of casino, and it says nothing in itself about how an operator behaves.

Are non GamStop casinos legal in the UK?

It is legal for you to play at one. The Gambling Act 2005 places its obligations on operators, not players: providing facilities for gambling without a licence is an offence committed by the operator, and there is no corresponding offence for a customer. What you lose is protection rather than legality — no Gambling Commission complaints route, no approved dispute-resolution body, no GamStop, and no requirement that customer funds be segregated.

Can I play at a non GamStop casino if I am self-excluded?

Technically these sites will let you register, because GamStop does not reach them. I would strongly advise against it. You self-excluded for a reason, and no welcome offer is worth overriding that decision for. If you want a block that holds regardless of licensing, use device-level software such as Gamban alongside your bank's gambling block, and speak to the National Gambling Helpline on 0808 8020 133 — free, confidential and available 24 hours a day.

Are casinos not on GamStop safe?

Less safe than a UK-licensed casino, and how much less depends on the operator and its regulator. You lose fund segregation requirements, the Commission's enforcement powers and access to an approved dispute-resolution body. A Malta licence is meaningfully supervised; the reformed Curaçao Gaming Control Board regime is improving; Anjouan is lighter touch. Verify the licence number on the regulator's own register before depositing, and treat an unverifiable licence as a reason not to.

Why do non GamStop casinos offer bigger bonuses?

Because UK advertising and bonus rules are strict and offshore ones are not. UK licensees face significant restrictions on how welcome offers can be structured and promoted; operators outside that system can offer 600% multi-deposit packages and hundreds of free spins. The generosity is real but the terms usually are not: on my modelling, most of these large offers cost more in expected losses than the bonus is worth. Each one is priced individually on the bonuses page.

Do I pay tax on winnings from a non GamStop casino?

No. Gambling winnings are not taxable income in the UK for recreational players, and the operator's licensing jurisdiction makes no difference to that. Gambling duty falls on operators rather than customers. There is no threshold to declare and nothing to enter on a self-assessment return. The only real complication is if you take winnings in cryptocurrency, where converting back to pounds can create a separate capital gains event.

Can I use my UK debit card at these casinos?

Often, but many UK banks decline gambling transactions to offshore merchants, and most banks offer a gambling block that many customers have switched on without remembering. Check your banking app's gambling settings first. Credit cards are separately banned for gambling in the UK and have been since April 2020. Crypto is the most reliable route both in and out, and by a wide margin the fastest on the way out.

What happens if a non GamStop casino refuses to pay me?

Your route is a complaint to the regulator named in the site's footer, and how much that is worth depends on which one it is. The UK Gambling Commission cannot act, because it does not license the operator. Practically: raise a formal complaint in writing with the operator first, keep every transcript and screenshot, then escalate to the regulator with that evidence. This is precisely why verifying the licence before depositing, and keeping balances small, matter more offshore than they do at home.
